- License: MIT, text below.
- Ported:
  - `app.js` is a port of `main.js`: the same DOM, controls, and polling. Its `/api` calls are answered in the page by `session.js` instead of a Python server. Multi-model comparison and connection settings are omitted.

## Franka Emika Panda (MuJoCo Menagerie)

- Source: [google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie `franka_emika_panda`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/822c2d8f877dd166c5b7d3c9f7e3c3b6589473b7/franka_emika_panda), commit `822c2d8f877dd166c5b7d3c9f7e3c3b6589473b7`, as vendored by EmbodiedJev.
- License: Apache-2.0. The full text is `vendor/panda/LICENSE`.
- `vendor/panda/panda.js` is **modified** and generated by `tools/vendor-panda.mjs`:
  - `panda.xml` (SHA-256 `96ad67da03710f17f798c9478fd9e9efdf24a3bf8359f05e456dd9fb158ea273`): model renamed, `meshdir` removed, timestep 0.002, collision friction `1.8 .02 .002`, visual geoms and their mesh assets removed from the physics model, keyframe removed, a `tcp` site added to the hand, and a marker added where the task scene is inserted.
  - The visual meshes are drawn by three.js instead. Their vertices are quantized to 16 bits per axis over each mesh's bounding box and packed with their indices.
  - The collision mesh files are unmodified.
  - Everything is gzip-compressed and base64-encoded in one module.

## LIBERO and robosuite

- Source: [Lifelong-Robot-Learning/LIBERO](https://github.com/Lifelong-Robot-Learning/LIBERO) at commit `8f1084e3132a39270c3a13ebe37270a43ece2a01`, libero_90 tasks 0 and 33 (scene, object models, textures, init states, and the `Close` predicate); [ARISE-Initiative/robosuite](https://github.com/ARISE-Initiative/robosuite) 1.4.1 (Panda robot, gripper, and mount models; OSC_POSE controller parameters).
- License: MIT for both. The full texts are `vendor/libero/LICENSE`.
- `vendor/libero/drawer.js` and `vendor/libero/microwave.js` are **modified** and generated by `tools/libero/` in the package source:
  - The physics MJCF is robosuite's compiled model of the task with every mesh written inline. Its legacy `njmax`/`nconmax` sizing is replaced by `<size memory="16M"/>`. Collision geoms keep their convex hulls; the export checks each against the original within 0.04 mm.
  - The episode is the task's init states 0–9, each after robosuite's settling steps, with the controller state and robosuite's camera calibration.
  - The visual meshes are simplified on the welded surface (each keeps its area within 3% of the source), quantized to 16 bits per axis, and packed with their indices. Textures are re-encoded as JPEG at most 512 px.
  - `libero-world.js` ports robosuite's OSC_POSE controller and Panda gripper to MuJoCo WASM.

- Source: npm package [`@mujoco/mujoco@3.15.0`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@mujoco/mujoco), built from [google-deepmind/mujoco `wasm/`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco/tree/main/wasm).
- Copyright: Google DeepMind.
- License: Apache-2.0. The full text is `vendor/mujoco/LICENSE`.
- Included: the single-threaded build.
  - `mujoco.wasm` is unmodified. It is carried gzip-compressed and base64-encoded in `vendor/mujoco/wasm.js`; `engine.js` restores the original bytes.
  - `vendor/mujoco/mujoco.js` is **modified**. The upstream file generates embind invokers with `new Function`, which the Desktop view policy refuses. Each change is marked `// a3s patch:`. `tools/vendor-mujoco.mjs` in the package source applies the patch to the upstream file.
